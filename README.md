# Pet Photographer AI Kit

*Working name.* Your pet photography business in one folder, so Claude or Codex knows your business, your client, your prices and your voice before you type a word.

Built by Ina Jalil (Ina J Education) for pet photographers. Run it in a live workshop, or step by step on your own.

## How it works

Every part of your business gets a folder. Every decision gets written into a file. Each step asks you questions one at a time, writes one file, and shows you what it saved. Each step reads the files from the steps before it, so the more you build, the less you re-explain.

## Start here

1. Install the Claude desktop app from [claude.ai/download](https://claude.ai/download). Sign in and open the **Code** tab.
2. Start a new session and paste: "Clone https://github.com/inajphotography/pet-photographer-ai-kit into my Documents folder, then open that folder so we can work in it."
3. Type `/setup-check` (in Codex, say "run the setup check step"). Green ticks mean you're ready.
4. Type `/context-file` (in Codex, "run the context file step"). This is step 1, and the most important one.

## The steps

| Step | Type | You end up with |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `/setup-check` | A laptop that's ready |
| 1 | `/context-file` | The file Claude reads first, every session |
| 2 | `/ideal-client` | Who you're for, in detail |
| 3 | `/offer-pricing` | Your real price list, and how booking works |
| 4 | `/voice` | How you sound, from a recording of you talking |
| 5 | `/brand-look` | Your colours, fonts and logo, written down |
| 6 | `/content-week` | Your first week of posts |
| 7 | `/enquiry-replies` | Draft replies to the enquiries you get most |
| 8 | `/security-check` | A check for leaked passwords and client details |

## The rules the kit follows

- It never invents a price, a client story or a testimonial. If it doesn't know, it asks.
- It drafts. It never sends, posts, books or charges anything.
- Passwords and keys never go in these files.
