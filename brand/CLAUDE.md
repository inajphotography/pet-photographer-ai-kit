# Brand

**Filled in by:** step 4 (`/voice`) and step 5 (`/brand-look`).

| Reads from | Feeds into |
|---|---|
| A transcript of you talking (a consult call or recorded video), your writing samples, your existing logo, colours and fonts, `business/context.md` | Everything written or designed in your name |

## Files

- `voice.md`: how you actually sound, taken from your real speech first and your writing second. If it's built from typed answers only, it's marked provisional until you add a transcript.
- `brand.md`: your look. Colours with exact hex codes, fonts, logo, layout. Taken from what you already use, never invented.
